# 90-glossary - The Working Vocabulary

Purpose: keep the words this vault uses meaning the same thing in every note, including the words the idea itself invents. A term that means two things in one vault produces two notes that appear to agree.

## What belongs here

A term that a note uses in a way a newcomer would not guess: the vault's own mechanism vocabulary (claim, register, handle, gate, userspace), and any term the idea introduces or redefines. One entry per term, with the definition and the note that owns the term.

## What does not belong here

A word used in its ordinary sense. A claim about the world: `20-claims/`. A debate about what a term should mean: an `OQ-###`, with the winning answer recorded here. A name for a part of the idea: `30-structure/`.

## Rules for this folder

- An entry is added when a term is first used in a non-obvious way, not collected in advance. A glossary written before the writing is a list of words nobody will use.
- One definition per term. If a note needs a second meaning, the term is qualified (`gate (verification)`), and the ambiguity is recorded as an open question.
- The entry names where the term is defined in full, so the definition is not maintained in two places.
- A term that stops being used is retired with a line saying what replaced it.
- Entries are descriptive: no normative wording.
