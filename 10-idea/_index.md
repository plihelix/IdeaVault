# 10-idea - The Idea As It Is Understood

Purpose: hold the idea in the form it currently has - a half-stated intention, a design sketch, a plan, a position, or a settled body of knowledge - so that the rest of the vault has something to be about, and so that "what we think now" is written down rather than remembered.

## What belongs here

- The statement: what the idea is, in the words you would use with a stranger.
- Its shape: what kind of thing it is, and therefore which domains the vault needs for it.
- Scope and non-goals: what this idea does not cover, written down so a later reader does not quietly add it.
- Who it is for and who it is not for.
- The version of the idea you are currently working against, when there is more than one.

## What does not belong here

Obligations, constraints, assumptions, and acceptance criteria: those are `20-claims/`. Parts and boundaries: `30-structure/`. Stages and roles: `40-plan/`. Doubts about whether it will work: `50-risk/`. Outside facts: `70-evidence/`. A question you cannot answer yet: an `OQ-###` in [01-userspace/questions/](../01-userspace/questions/_index.md).

## Rules for this folder

- Notes here are descriptive: handles of the form `IDEA-<TOPIC>`, no numbered namespace, no normative wording.
- A note in this folder may be `draft` indefinitely. An idea that is still forming is not a defect; a note that pretends it is settled is.
- When the idea changes shape, the note is superseded, not edited in place: the earlier understanding is what the existing claims were written against.
- The folder holds one primary statement note. Everything else here qualifies, scopes, or contradicts it.
- A change here that closes an option is a decision record in [01-userspace/decisions/](../01-userspace/decisions/_index.md), not an edit.
