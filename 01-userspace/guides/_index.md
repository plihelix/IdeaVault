# 01-userspace/guides - How to Use This Vault

Purpose: instructions written for the person who owns the vault - how to start, how to read and write notes, how to reshape the folders around the idea, how to work alongside an agent, and how to check that the vault is telling the truth about itself.

## Rules for this folder

- A guide routes to a mechanism note; it never restates one. If a guide and a mechanism note disagree, the mechanism note is right and the guide is a defect.
- Guides belong to the owner: they carry the owner's name in `owner:` and the owner edits them. A guide you have edited is your instruction to the agent.
- A guide is `type: note` and describes. Normative wording lives in `20-claims/`, not here.
- A guide answers a question someone actually had. When a question stops being asked, retire the guide rather than growing the set.

## Evidence

None: guides describe this vault's own mechanism, which is stated in [00-vault/](../../00-vault/_index.md) rather than evidenced.
