# OQ-001: What idea will this vault hold

Status: draft, open since 2026-10-03.

## Question

Which idea is this vault for: one intention the owner is carrying, one thing they are trying to build or write, one position they are forming, or a body of knowledge they are settling?

## Blocks

Everything that depends on the subject. `10-idea/` cannot be written, so no claim in `20-claims/` can be named, no part in `30-structure/` identified, no stage in `40-plan/` sequenced, no risk in `50-risk/` assessed, no gate in `60-quality/` set, and no credibility standard in `70-evidence/` chosen. [ADR-0001](../decisions/adr-0001-the-template-holds-any-idea-in-generic-domains.md) ships a domain set that this answer may narrow, and the output kinds in [ADR-0004](../decisions/adr-0004-the-vault-issues-standalone-outputs-in-declared-kinds.md) are chosen against a subject this question has not named.

## Answered by

The owner. No lookup and no measurement can answer it: it is the owner's intention, and the vault has no standing to guess.

## How it would be answered

Write one paragraph in `10-idea/` saying what the idea is, in the terms the owner would use with a stranger, and one paragraph saying what would count as this idea succeeding. If the paragraph cannot be written, the honest answer is that the vault is holding an intention that is not yet an idea, and that is written in `10-idea/` as `draft` rather than left as an empty folder.

## Due

2026-10-17, set 2026-10-03. The date is a prompt, not a deadline imposed by anyone else: if it passes unanswered, the answer is written as `draft` and the date is moved with a line saying why.

## Answer

Nothing tried yet. The vault ships with the mechanism complete and the subject absent, which is the state this question exists to end.

## Evidence

None available: the question is about the owner's intention, and no outside source can supply it.
